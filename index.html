How it works

Four questions, then your book.

Each answer changes what we ask next. Pick Science and we ask which class and which board. Pick Engineering and we ask which branch. Nobody answers a question that doesn't apply to them.

1

Tell us about you

Your name, and whether you're at school, in college, preparing for an exam or learning for work. This sets the tone of every chapter.

2

Narrow the field

Area, then branch, then the follow-up questions that actually matter — board, class, semester, exam paper or job role.

3

Read your book

A contents page, then chapters with topic headings, explanations, worked examples and margin notes. Exactly like the textbook you wish you had.

4

Test each chapter

Five questions at the end of every chapter, marked the moment you answer, with an explanation for each one you miss.

Inside a Skillbook

Not a chatbot transcript.
An actual book.

Answers you have to keep re-asking for aren't learning. Skillbook gives you something with a beginning, a middle and an end.

A contents page you can plan around

Every chapter titled and timed before you start, so you know what the whole journey looks like.

Worked examples, not just explanations

Each concept lands with a full example — the problem, every step, and why that step was the right one.

Tests that mark themselves

Five questions per chapter, scored live, with an explanation for every answer you get wrong.

Search inside your book

Look up any topic and jump straight to the page that covers it — the way you'd use an index.
